What's inside

Everything a
top agent
actually needs

Most CRMs were built for sales teams, not real estate agents. Emlak CRM was designed from the ground up for agents managing mixed rental and sale portfolios.
Property Portfolio
Manage rental and sale listings side-by-side. Track status, link owners, and attach up to 10 photos per property.
Smart Contracts
Generate professional lease and sale contracts as PDFs. Auto-detect overlap conflicts and set rent increase reminders.
Commission Tracker
Log rental and sale commissions separately. See what you've earned, what's pending, and project future income.
Financial Ledger
Track income, expenses, and recurring costs. Multi-currency support with live exchange rates and receipt upload.
Client Management
Owners, tenants, and buyer leads in one view. Duplicate detection prevents double-booking and data conflicts.
Calendar & Showings
Schedule property showings, client meetings, and follow-ups. Get reminders before every appointment.
