**Date:** January 2025

**Research Scope:** Industry best practices, KPIs, and financial metrics for commission-based real estate businesses

---

## Executive Summary

This report identifies the most critical analytics and metrics that estate agents need in their finance dashboard. The research reveals that estate agents require a combination of **commission tracking**, **operational efficiency metrics**, and **business health indicators** to make data-driven decisions.

**Key Finding:** Estate agents operate on a commission-based model, so analytics should focus on:
1. **Commission Performance** (GCI, average per transaction, trends)
2. **Operational Efficiency** (expense ratios, net income, cash flow)
3. **Business Growth** (conversion rates, transaction volume, trends)

## 1. CRITICAL METRICS FOR ESTATE AGENTS

### 1.1 Commission-Based Revenue Metrics

#### **Gross Commission Income (GCI)**
- **Definition:** Total revenue from commissions before expenses
- **Why Critical:** Primary measure of financial success
- **Industry Benchmark:** Median GCI for REALTORS® was $56,400 in 2022
- **Current Status:** ✅ Already tracked (Total Income/Revenue)
- **Recommendation:** Keep as primary metric, ensure it's prominently displayed

#### **Average Commission per Transaction**
- **Definition:** Typical commission earned per closed deal
- **Why Critical:** Reflects negotiation skills and market segment focus

- **Current Status:** ✅ Already tracked (Monthly Commission Trends card)

### 1.2 Operational Efficiency Metrics

- **Current Status:** ✅ Already tracked (Profit in dashboard)
- **Recommendation:** Keep, ensure it's clearly visible

#### **Expense Ratio**
- **Definition:** Operating expenses as percentage of revenue
- **Why Critical:** Helps agents manage costs and improve efficiency
- **Current Status:** ✅ Already calculated but not prominently displayed
- **Recommendation:** Add as a KPI card or include in ratios section

- **Definition:** (Net Income / Total Revenue) × 100
