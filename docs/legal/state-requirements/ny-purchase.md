| Topic | Understanding | Uncertainty |

## 3. Attorney review — FLAG (NY-specific)

- New York residential practice is **attorney-centric**: contracts are commonly drafted/negotiated by attorneys **before** the deal is binding.
- Unlike New Jersey’s classic post-signature attorney-review window, many NY sources emphasize **pre-signature attorney negotiation**; some brokers still use attorney-approval clauses — practice varies by region.
- **V1 product requirement:** NY purchase drafts must include a prominent notice that parties should have New York real estate counsel review/negotiate before relying on the document, and that this draft is **not** a substitute for attorney contract practice.
