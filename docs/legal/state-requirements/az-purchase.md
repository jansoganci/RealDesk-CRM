# Arizona — Residential Purchase (research summary)

**Status:** Phase 1 research only · Not attorney-reviewed · Not an AAR Residential Resale Contract  
**Sources consulted:** A.R.S. §33-422 (Affidavit of Disclosure for certain unincorporated land divisions); secondary materials on seller material-fact disclosure duty and AAR SPDS market practice (noted as practice, not copied).

## 1. Mandatory disclosures

| Topic | Understanding | Uncertainty |
|-------|---------------|-------------|
| Material facts | Arizona sellers generally must disclose known material facts (case law + practice) | No single statewide statutory “TDS” identical to CA |
| SPDS (market practice) | AAR contracts commonly require Seller’s Property Disclosure Statement delivery in ~3 days — **association practice**, not our form | Mention obligation to provide a complete seller property disclosure; do not copy AAR SPDS |
| Affidavit of Disclosure (§33-422) | Certain sales of ≤5 parcels in unincorporated areas (non-subdivided) require statutory affidavit; buyer rescission window after receipt | Applicability needs parcel/subdivision facts — **wizard gap** |
| Lead paint | Federal | Modeled |
