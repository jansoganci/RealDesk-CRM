2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. Cookies enable websites to remember your visit, maintain your session, and store your preferences.

2.1. Types of Cookies

By duration:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: Cookies that remain on your device until they expire or are manually deleted.

By origin:

  • First-Party Cookies: Set directly by the website you are visiting.
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by a different domain (e.g., Google) than the website you are visiting.

By purpose:

  • Essential (Technical) Cookies: Strictly necessary for the website to function.
  • Analytics/Performance Cookies: Used to analyze website usage and performance.

3. Cookies Used on Our Website

9. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under GDPR Chapter III, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): You have the right to obtain confirmation as to whether or not your personal data is being processed.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): You have the right to have inaccurate personal data corrected.
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): You have the right to have your personal data deleted ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Article 18): You have the right to restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Article 21): You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 7): You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint (Article 77): You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
