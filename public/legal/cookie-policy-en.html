1. Introduction

The Real Estate CRM platform (operated by Jans Trade, LLC) uses cookies and similar technologies to provide our services and improve user experience. This Cookie Policy explains which cookies are used, why they are used, and how you can manage them.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are saved on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. Cookies allow the website to remember your visit, keep you logged in, and store your preferences.

3. Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of the Platform. They enable you to navigate the site and use its features, such as accessing secure areas.

  • Authentication: Used by Supabase to verify your identity and keep you logged in securely.
  • Security: Used to prevent Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) and other attacks.
  • Preferences: Used to remember your language selection (e.g., 'i18nextLng').

Status: Active (Cannot be disabled)

Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help understand how users interact with the website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

  • Analytics: We do not currently use third-party analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Hotjar).

Status: Not Currently Used

Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to track visitors across websites to display relevant ads.

Status: Not Used (We do not display ads or track you for advertising purposes).

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some features of our Platform rely on third-party services which may set their own cookies:

  • Supabase: Our backend provider uses identifiers to manage your secure session.
  • Google (OAuth): If you choose to sign in with Google, Google may set cookies on your browser to authenticate your Google account. We do not control these cookies.

For more information, please review the Supabase Privacy Policy and Google Cookie Policy.

5. Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. However, because we only use strictly necessary cookies, blocking them will likely prevent you from logging in or using the Platform effectively.

Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to view, manage, delete, and block cookies for a website. You can typically find these controls in your browser's "Settings" or "Privacy" menu.
